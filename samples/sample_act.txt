Section 12. Limitation on transfer.

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in section 9, no person shall transfer any specified asset without the prior written approval of the Authority.

(2) Subject to the provisions of sub-section (1), a transfer made in the ordinary course of business shall be permitted.

Provided that nothing in this section shall apply to a transfer effected by operation of law.

Provided further that the Authority may, for reasons to be recorded in writing, exempt any class of transfers.

Explanation.—For the purposes of this section, "specified asset" means any immovable property held for more than three years, and includes any leasehold interest therein.

Illustration.—A holds land for four years and leases it to B. The lease is a specified asset.

Section 13. Repeal and savings.

(1) The Transfer Regulation Act, 1998 is hereby repealed.

(2) Save as otherwise provided, anything done under the repealed Act shall be deemed to have been done under this Act.

In Kesavan v. State (2019) 4 SCC 221, this Court held that the phrase must be read narrowly. That view was overruled in Mehta v. Union of India, 2023 INSC 118, which held it was decided per incuriam.
